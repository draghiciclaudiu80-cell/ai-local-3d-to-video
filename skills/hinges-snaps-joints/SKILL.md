Hinge, printed in place (one print, moves right away):
- Split the length into 3 or 5 knuckles; the middle one(s) belong to part A, the outer ones to part B. Between knuckles 0.4-0.5 mm; around the pin 0.4 mm.
- Pin radius >= 1.5 mm, fused to part A's middle knuckle; part B's knuckles have holes = pin + 0.4. Knuckle radius = pin + 2.5 mm at least.
- Connect each knuckle to its own part with a web; keep 0.5 mm clearance from the OTHER part all around, or it can't turn.
Hinge from separate parts: same but the pin is a bought 3 mm rod / M3 screw; holes 3.2 mm (turning) or 2.9 mm (press fit).

Snap-fit clip (cantilever): arm length >= 5 x its thickness; thickness 1.2-2 mm; hook 0.6-1 mm deep with a 30-45 degree lead-in; print the arm lying flat (bends along the layers break). PLA: strain under 2 % — longer arm = safer.
Detent (clicks into positions): a thin flexure arm (1.2-1.8 mm, 12+ mm long) with a 1.5 mm ball nub; dimples 0.4 mm bigger than the nub.
Living hinge: only in PP/TPU — never PLA. Use a pin hinge instead.
Press fit: 0.1-0.2 mm tighter than the hole; sliding fit: 0.3-0.4 mm looser; glued: 0.2-0.3 mm gap.
Lids: a lip 3-5 mm deep, 0.3 mm smaller than the opening on every side.
Chains printed in place: rounded rectangular links (not round tori — round links can't interlock without touching), alternate flat / standing, 0.8-1 mm gaps, pitch ~ hole length.
Screws: M3 into plastic = 2.5 mm hole (self-tapping) or 3.2 mm clearance + nut trap (5.7 mm across flats, 2.6 mm deep).
